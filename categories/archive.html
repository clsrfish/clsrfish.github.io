Archive

Anything from the other three categories that is no longer active, but you might want to save for future reference. Inactive, not bad. The standard cases:

  • Completed projects — the work is done; the project shipped or wrapped up.
  • Deactivated areas — a responsibility you no longer maintain (a role you left, a topic you no longer track).
  • Lapsed resources — a topic you’re no longer interested in, or material that’s been superseded by newer thinking.

Nothing is deleted; Archive is cold storage. Notes remain searchable and reference-able, just demoted from active rotation. Bases by default exclude Archive from primary views — see Notes.base.
